# When have you felt happiest at work? And when have you felt the least fulfilled? We’d love to understand the kinds of work, environments, or challenges that help you thrive  – and what tends to have the opposite effect.

I am the least fulfilled when working where success metrics are unclear, where my perspective/contributions don't matter, and where the team interfaces with each other robotically. (We don't have to be best friends, but gosh darn, we're all super human aren't we?)

I am most fulfilled when
- the whole team is invested, understanding the short term objectives in the context of our long term value proposition and ethos
- the team is commited to the excellent results and maintains high-trust, high-support experince
- we get to create cool things, meet new people, learn about different companies and industries, become early-adopters (at least some of the time!) of emergent tech
